Vault Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Vault Tracker is published by 2351485 ALBERTA INC. (operating as AIMLDS). This policy describes what the Vault Tracker app does with your information. It is short because the app is built to collect nothing.

No account

Vault Tracker has no sign-up and no user accounts. You create a vault on your own device, protected by a password that only you know. We never receive your password or your keys.

No data collected, nothing shared

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Your entries never leave your device through the app.

Stored on your device, encrypted

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. Item contents are additionally encrypted with keys derived from your vault password. If you delete the app or your vault, that data is gone — we hold no copy and cannot recover it for you.

Exports and backups are your files

If you choose to export or back up your vault, the app creates a file that you save or share yourself, to a place you choose. That is the only way vault data leaves the app, and it happens only when you initiate it.

Permissions

The Android app declares no INTERNET permission and works fully offline. The app makes no network requests of its own and sends no data anywhere. Links shown inside the app (for example, to the project page) open in your browser only when you tap them.
